# AgriDistro: B2B Garlic, Ayurvedic Seeds & Pulses Distribution App

A mobile-first web app built specifically for mobile phones and smartphones, designed for agricultural commodity distributors and wholesalers supplying fresh garlic, ayurvedic seeds (Kalonji, Methi, Ajwain, Jeera), and pulses to local grocery stores and marts.

---

## 📱 What's New in this Mobile Update

### 1. 🏷️ Price in Ranges (e.g. ₹240 - ₹260 / kg)
- Prices in mandis fluctuate by quality, size, and lot. You can now set a **Min Rate** and **Max Rate** for any product.
- App displays clean range badges: `₹240 - ₹260 / kg`.
- **WhatsApp Broadcast:** Automatically formats prices as `Rate: ₹240 - ₹260 / kg (MOQ: 50 kg Bag)`.
- **Delivery Logging:** When recording a delivery, it shows today's range with 1-tap quick buttons: `[Min: ₹240]`, `[Mid: ₹250]`, and `[Max: ₹260]`, plus allows typing any custom agreed price.

### 2. ➕ Add & Manage Products Manually
- Add any new garlic variety, ayurvedic seed, spice, or pulse on the fly with the **"➕ Add Item"** button.
- Choose custom name, category (Garlic, Seeds, Pulses, Herbs, Spices), custom icon/emoji, unit (`kg`, `50kg Bag`, `Quintal`, `25kg Sack`), MOQ, and min/max rates.
- Edit or delete products whenever you need.

### 3. 📷 Change Business Logo Yourself
- Tap the logo icon in the top header or in Settings to upload your own firm or shop logo directly from your phone's photo gallery or camera.
- Also includes ready-to-use commodity icons (🧄, 🌱, 🥣, 🌿, 🌾, 🚜).
- Your custom logo automatically updates across:
  - Top Mobile Header
  - Official Digital Delivery Challan / Bilty
  - WhatsApp Rate Card header
  - Public Storefront Catalog

### 4. 📱 Native Smartphone (Phone-First) Design
- Designed specifically for one-handed thumb use on Android and iPhone:
  - Fixed bottom navigation bar (`📊 Rates`, `🚚 Supply`, `📒 Khata`, `📱 Storefront`, `⚙️ Settings`).
  - Mobile bottom-sheet modals that slide up smoothly.
  - Large tap targets and touch-friendly number pads.
  - 1-tap WhatsApp sharing to grocery store owners' numbers.
